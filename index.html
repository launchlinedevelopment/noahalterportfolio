01 · ABOUT

I like building things from the ground up.

I’m Noah Alter, a student at Colts Neck High School with a strong interest in business, entrepreneurship, accounting, leadership, and community service. I’m especially interested in how accounting turns the activity behind a business into a clear picture of what is actually working.

That has meant starting a nonprofit, creating an apparel brand, leading within BBYO, volunteering as a junior firefighter, competing in DECA, and working with kids in my community.

Each experience has taught me something different about leadership. I’ve learned that leading is not always about being the loudest person in the room. It is about bringing people together, taking responsibility, and turning ideas into action.

I’d rather build something imperfect than wait forever for the perfect idea.
MY OPERATING SYSTEM

Three rules I keep coming back to.

01

Start before it feels finished.

The fastest way I learn is by making something real, showing it to people, and improving it from there.

02

People make the idea matter.

A program, brand, or organization only becomes meaningful when other people feel like they belong in it.

03

Do the unglamorous part too.

Planning, follow-up, logistics, revisions, cleanup — the work behind the work is usually what makes the visible part possible.
